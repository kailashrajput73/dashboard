# Dispatch & Billing

Use this screen to review past dispatches, bill walk-in retail customers, or dispatch an approved RFQ.

## Review dispatches

- Search by product, customer, dispatch ID, or RFQ.
- Choose All, Retail, or RFQ-linked.
- Enter From and To dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` format to filter by dispatch date.
- The summary reflects the filtered records: dispatches, lines, total quantity, and value.
- Select a row to expand its product lines, customer details, and RFQ link.
- Use the download icon to export the filtered line items to CSV.

## Retail billing

Select Retail customer billing or the plus icon. Search by product name, code, or brand, or enter a product code, then add each product to the cart. Enter a positive quantity; repeated products are combined. Adjust cart quantities or remove lines. The cart shows current stock, line prices, and total value. Add an optional customer name and phone, then select Bill and dispatch. The system blocks dispatch when cart quantity exceeds the shown stock.

## Dispatch an approved RFQ

Select Dispatch approved RFQ, review the partner and product lines, then select Dispatch for a request with sufficient stock. Missing catalog products or insufficient stock block the action.

Dispatching deducts stock. This screen does not provide a reversal or undo operation; verify quantities before billing or dispatching.
