# Referral Partners

The Partners screen lists partner accounts and their KYC status. Search by name, phone, pincode, city, or area. Select a status filter or a sales-manager filter to narrow the list. The partner export downloads the currently filtered partners as `partners.csv`. The KYC-history export downloads one row per history entry for those partners as `partners-kyc-history.csv`; partners without history have no rows in that file.

## Partner details

Select a partner to review business/contact/location details, app activity, registration and login information, reward balance, sales performance, documents, KYC review history, reward passbook, and purchase history. HTTP(S) document URLs open in a new browser tab; other document paths are displayed as text.

## Review pending KYC

Pending partners show the review controls. Set **Location verified** as appropriate. **Approve KYC** approves the partner with that location flag. To reject, enter a reason first, then select **Reject**. The reason is required and appears in the review history.

## Add a partner

Select **Add partner** and enter the required name and phone. Address, business name, pincode, city, area, sales manager, and document URLs/paths are optional. Separate multiple documents with commas or line breaks. Admin-created partners are approved automatically; this form does not create mobile login credentials. The new partner is opened in details after creation.

Partner creation and KYC review affect live partner records. Verify the selected record before submitting; a rejection reason and review are retained in partner history.
