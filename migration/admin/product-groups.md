# Product Groups

Use Product Groups to organize related catalog products and manage their group prices.

- Search by group name. The count below the title shows the filtered and total groups.
- Select **New product group**, enter a group name, search the catalog, and select at least two products. Select **Create group** to save.
- To edit, select the edit icon, update the name or selected products, then select **Save changes**. A group must contain at least two products.
- Select the product count to expand the group. When the shelf price board is enabled, it shows grouped sizes with individual price editing and a group-wide discount. Otherwise, the linked products are listed.
- To delete a group and its products, select the delete icon and confirm with the admin contact number and passcode. This cascade removes the group and its products; check the confirmation message for the number removed.
