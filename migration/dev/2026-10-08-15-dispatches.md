# Row 15: Dispatches

**Planned:** Port dispatch history, retail customer billing, and dispatching approved RFQs.

**Built:** Added the Dispatch & Billing page with search, retail/RFQ filters, date bounds, summary, filtered line-level CSV export, and expandable dispatch details. Retail billing supports catalog search/code lookup, a multi-line cart, quantity edits, optional customer details, stock validation, and dispatch. Approved RFQs can be dispatched from a separate modal after checking stock.

**Old -> new:** `frontend/app/(admin)/dispatches.tsx` -> `web/src/pages/DispatchesPage.tsx`; `/dispatches` route updated in `web/src/App.tsx`.

**How it works:** The page loads dispatches, catalog, partners, and RFQs. Both retail and RFQ dispatch use the existing `createDispatch` request; retail sends optional customer fields, and RFQ dispatch includes its source RFQ ID.

**API calls:** `GET /dispatches`, `GET /catalog`, `GET /partners`, `GET /rfqs`, `POST /dispatches`.

**Differences from Expo:** React controls and the browser CSV helper replace React Native controls and platform export. The data fields, filters, stock checks, and dispatch payloads follow Expo.

**How to test:** Filter dispatches by text, type, and date; expand a dispatch and check line/customer/RFQ details; export the filtered rows. Build a retail cart, verify total and stock validation, and dispatch. Open approved RFQs, dispatch one with sufficient stock, and verify the list and stock refresh. Run `npm run build` from `web/`.

**Caution:** Billing or dispatching reduces stock. There is no undo action in this screen.
